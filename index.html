      About the Project

      CyberSafe U is a student project about security awareness. It compares how well students answer questions about phishing, password security, online scams and two-factor authentication.

      The data in this dashboard is SIMULATED. The Student IDs are made up and the answers were generated by the program. They are not actual student responses.

      The six supplementary tests (privacy, malware, social media, behavior, and general knowledge) are extra modules. The main research analysis stays focused on the four original proposal topics.
