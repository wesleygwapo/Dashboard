Project Overview

Project title: Cybersecurity Awareness Among Students of USeP Obrero Campus

Information security domain: Security Awareness

Information Security Problem

This project aims to assess the cybersecurity awareness of students at the University of Southeastern Philippines (USeP) Obrero Campus. It will examine their knowledge of online threats and security practices, such as phishing, password security, online scams, and two-factor authentication. The results will help determine how well students understand these cybersecurity topics.

Dataset & Variables

The project will use a simulated dataset containing answers from 30 USeP Obrero Campus students to cybersecurity awareness questions covering phishing, password security, online scams, and two-factor authentication.

Variables: Student ID, cybersecurity topic, question, and answer correctness (correct or incorrect).

Research Question

Which cybersecurity topic has the lowest percentage of correct answers among students of USeP Obrero Campus?

Statistical Plan

Frequency counts and percentages will show how many students answered correctly and incorrectly in each topic. A bar chart will compare topic results and identify which topic has the lowest percentage of correct answers.

Dashboard Plan

The website dashboard will present and analyze cybersecurity awareness test results for USeP Obrero Campus students. It will summarize the number of students, correct and incorrect answers, and quiz scores. Topic performance comparisons, correct/incorrect distributions, question analysis, student performance, topic rankings, knowledge gaps, recommendations, dataset records, and project methods will be presented with charts and tables.

Recommendations

Based on test results, the project will recommend additional lessons, practical activities, and awareness campaigns focused on topics with the lowest percentages of correct answers. These recommendations will help students improve their knowledge of phishing, password security, online scams, and two-factor authentication.

    CyberSafe U summarizes 53 complete survey submissions from supplied workbooks. One source tab is explicitly labeled “Simulated Responses.”

    There are 51 unique reported student IDs across five colleges. Two additional complete submissions have no ID or college and remain included as unassigned records.

    Response frequencies and self-reported practices are calculated from original answers. Correctness and scores are not inferred without an answer key or coding rubric. The supplemental workbook has no separate interview questions.
